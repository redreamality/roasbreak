Profit Lever Calculator

Compare one-variable changes and see which one creates the most acquisition room at your current economics.

Read it correctly

A sensitivity ranking, not a forecast.

The calculator changes AOV, product cost, fulfillment, fees, or returns one at a time. That makes the economic effect visible without claiming that the business can achieve the change or that other metrics will stay fixed.

Why single-variable scenarios?

Raising price may lower conversion. Free shipping may increase AOV while increasing fulfillment cost. Combining assumptions automatically would hide these relationships, so this page ranks isolated moves first.

Use the ranking to form a test

The highest row is the change that adds the most target CPA room under the stated assumptions. It is a place to investigate, negotiate, or experiment, not an instruction generated from industry averages.
