ROAS Scenario Planner

Put product, channel, and spend assumptions side by side and rank them by advertising contribution profit.

A planning model

Higher ROAS does not always mean higher total profit.

A smaller campaign can report a stronger ROAS while producing less total contribution than a larger, less efficient campaign. This planner makes spend, revenue, orders, and contribution visible together.

Every scenario is an explicit assumption. Enter the ROAS you believe each spend level can sustain; the planner does not infer an optimal budget or assume efficiency remains unchanged during scaling.
