Two views of marketing efficiency

Attributed ROAS vs MER

Attributed ROAS uses revenue credited by one platform. MER uses total store revenue and total marketing spend. Neither is automatically incremental profit.

Do not add attribution claims

Each platform can claim the same order.

MetricNumeratorDenominatorBest use
Attributed ROASPlatform-credited revenueThat platform's spendCampaign optimization inside one attribution system
MER / blended ROASTotal store revenueTotal marketing spendStore-wide efficiency trend

Platform revenue can include view-through or click-through credit and use a different window from Shopify or GA4. Adding Meta, Google, and TikTok attributed revenue can exceed actual store sales because attribution credit is not exclusive.

Example

A store records $100,000 in net sales and spends $25,000 across channels, so MER is 4.0x. If Google claims $55,000 and Meta claims $65,000, their attributed revenue totals $120,000. That does not mean the store generated $120,000.

Use a consistent revenue basis and mature date range. Compare each platform to its own historical performance, and use store-wide sales and spend for blended efficiency.
