Name the numerator

Ecommerce Revenue Basis for ROAS

AOV, gross sales, net sales, total sales, and platform conversion value can all contain different things. The denominator cannot repair a mismatched numerator.

Recommended basis

Use net product revenue, excluding tax.

For an order-level profitability model, start with product revenue after discounts and expected refunds or sales reversals. Exclude tax because it is collected for remittance. Treat shipping revenue and shipping cost explicitly instead of hiding them in AOV.

ItemRecommended treatmentWhy
DiscountDeduct from product revenueThe customer never pays list price
Refund / sales reversalDeduct when matureReported purchase value can change later
TaxExcludeIt is not operating revenue
Shipping paid by customerShow separatelyIt offsets fulfillment but is not product revenue
ChargebackModel separatelyIt may not appear in a sales report

Why Shopify reports can disagree

Shopify sales, marketing, and finance reports use different definitions and update patterns. Marketing AOV may include tax and shipping and occur before returns, while net sales adjusts gross sales for discounts and sales reversals.

Label the source, model, and date range whenever a value is copied into a calculator. Do not call every revenue field “actual revenue.”
