ROAS vs ACoS

ROAS divides ad revenue by spend. ACoS divides spend by ad revenue. They use the same two numbers in reverse.

Break-even ACoS

At break-even, the entire contribution margin is available for advertising. A 40% contribution margin therefore means a 40% break-even ACoS, equivalent to 2.50x ROAS.

A target that retains profit must allow less ad spend: retaining 10% profit from the same order reduces target ACoS to 30% and raises target ROAS to 3.33x.

Amazon Ads defines ACoS as ad spend divided by ad revenue and ROAS as the inverse. Source: Amazon Ads ACoS guide.
