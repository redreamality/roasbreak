About ROAS Break

ROAS Break turns ecommerce unit economics and platform definitions into practical thresholds without pretending attributed revenue is the same as profit.

What we publish

ROAS Break is an independent collection of free calculators and original decision guides for ecommerce operators and paid media teams. It is published and maintained by floatboat.ai.

The tools cover break-even and target ROAS, allowable acquisition cost, promotion thresholds, CAC payback, and scenario-level contribution profit. The guides explain how to source the inputs, interpret platform metrics, reproduce each example, and recognize where the model stops.

How the work is built

Each result uses formulas shown or explained on the relevant page. We favor contribution economics over headline revenue, cite primary platform documentation, show a review date, and separate source facts from ROAS Break modeling choices.

The complete revenue, cost, attribution, rounding, example, and source rules are published in the methodology. Calculations run in your browser. Scenario values may be represented in the page URL so you can restore or share them; do not enter personal or confidential information.

Editorial independence

ROAS Break is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, Meta, Amazon, Shopify, TikTok, or other platforms referenced in its guides. Platform names belong to their respective owners.
