Privacy Policy

This policy explains what ROAS Break processes, why it is processed, and the choices available to visitors.

Publisher and contact

ROAS Break is published by floatboat.ai. For privacy questions or requests, email contact@floatboat.ai.

Calculator inputs

Calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not submitted to a ROAS Break account or database. Some tools place scenario values in the page URL so the result can be restored or shared. Those URLs may appear in browser history, server logs, or messages when you share them. Analytics page locations and referrers are configured without URL query strings. Do not enter names, email addresses, order IDs, customer data, or other personal or confidential information into calculator fields.

Hosting and security logs

Our hosting and content delivery providers may process standard request data such as IP address, requested URL, timestamp, browser or device information, and security signals to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and diagnose failures. These records are subject to the provider's retention and security practices.

Optional Google Analytics

ROAS Break offers optional Google Analytics. It is not loaded unless you select “Accept analytics.” If accepted, Google may place or read cookies and process IP addresses, device/browser information, page URLs, referrers, and interaction events to produce aggregated usage reports. We request IP anonymization and do not intentionally send names, email addresses, or other directly identifying data to Google Analytics.

You can reject optional analytics and continue using every calculator. Your choice is stored in local storage under roasbreak-privacy-choice. If you withdraw an earlier acceptance, ROAS Break immediately disables Analytics for the current page, removes its Analytics loader and event queue, and deletes the _ga cookies available to this site. Clear site data or use the button below to make the choice again.

Learn more at Google's Privacy Policy and How Google Analytics safeguards data.

Advertising services

ROAS Break may use Google AdSense or another advertising service in the future. If advertising is enabled, Google and other authorized third parties may place or read cookies, use web beacons, collect IP addresses or other identifiers, and process information about visits to serve, measure, and protect ads. Where required, we will request consent through an appropriate consent management platform before personalized advertising is used.

Google explains its advertising data practices at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Visitors can review or control Google ad personalization at My Ad Center.

Sensitive and children's data

ROAS Break is designed for business users and is not directed to children. We do not ask for precise location, health data, financial hardship data, government identifiers, or other sensitive personal information. Do not put such information into calculator fields or shared URLs.

Your choices and policy changes

You can reject optional analytics, clear local site data, or contact us about a privacy request. We may update this policy when services or legal requirements change; the effective date at the top will be updated for material revisions.
